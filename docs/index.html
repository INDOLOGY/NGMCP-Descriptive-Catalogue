NGMCP Descriptive Catalogue

Descriptions of nearly 16,000 manuscripts filmed by the Nepal-German Manuscript Preservation Project and catalogued by the Nepal-German Manuscript Cataloguing Project (1970–2014), many with excerpts of the beginning, end and colophon. This is a mirror of the data archived by Hamburg University in 2025.

Diacritics are optional: pasupati finds Paśupati. All words must match; put a phrase in "double quotes" to keep its words together.
